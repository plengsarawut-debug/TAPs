About Teachers as Practitioners

The Project

Creative lives,
beyond working lives.

Thirty-one teachers and business staff of Bangkok Patana School share the art they make beside their working lives: photography, painting, embroidery, sculpture, music, writing and performance.

Curated by Purnima Ruanglertbutr, this is the first international exhibition of the Teachers as Practitioners project, which began as research at Melbourne University in 2012.

2012

Project origins
Melbourne University
